How to Join

  1. Open Minecraft (Java or Bedrock Edition).
  2. Navigate to Multiplayer and click Add Server.
  3. Enter either meteorsmp.abrdns.com or ram-xs.tun.ply.gg.
  4. Hit connect and jump straight into the survival world!

How to Make Money

  • Sell Items: Use /sell to quickly earn cash from gathered items.
  • Server Shop: Use /shop to buy and sell resources.
  • Crates & Keys: Open keys acquired via /keys for high-value rewards.
  • Player Economy: Trade rare items or services with others using /pay.

How to Donate Items to Friends

  1. Meet Up with Your Friend: Use /tpa <player> to send a teleport request to your friend, or use /tpahere <player> to bring them directly to your location.
  2. Prepare Your Inventory: Gather the items, blocks, or gear you want to give away. If your items are stored safely away, use /pv to access your player vaults.
  3. Transfer the Items: Drop the items securely on the ground right next to your friend so they can pick them up.
  4. Send Money Support (Optional): If you also want to donate cash alongside your items, use the /pay <player> <amount> command.
