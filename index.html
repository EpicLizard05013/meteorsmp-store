How to Join

  1. Open Minecraft (Java or Bedrock Edition).
  2. Navigate to Multiplayer and click Add Server.
  3. Enter meteorsmp.abrdns.com.
  4. Hit connect and jump straight into the survival world!

How to Make Money

  • Sell Items: Use /sell to quickly earn cash from gathered items.
  • Server Shop: Use /shop to buy and sell resources.
  • Crates & Keys: Open keys acquired via /keys for high-value rewards.
  • Player Economy: Trade rare items or services using /pay.

Store Redemption Guide

  1. Select your item on the store, then run /redeem <code> in-game.
  2. Use /pay server <amount> to complete the in-game payment cost.
  3. Enjoy your rewards!

How to Gift Friends

  1. Select an item you want to gift your friend from the store.
  2. Use /gift-redeem <code> <discount> <friend> in-game.
  3. Use /pay server <amount> to complete the purchase.
